**The workspace looks like the desktop app.** In `stui --workspace`:

- **Toggles:** `ctrl-] b` toggles the sidebar and `ctrl-] p` the panel, and the menu bar's `[<]` and `[>]` do the same by mouse. Below 80 columns both start closed. What you pick is kept.
- **The sidebar is the orchestration tree.** The machine row comes first. Windows show their command's icon, and a coordinator with workers is marked `coordinator`. A worker in another session shows that session's name. Workers stay in view when you fold their lead's session. `+ New session` is the last row.
- **The right panel has seven icon tabs**, in the desktop's order. The picked tab's name is the title under the row. `ctrl-] 1`–`7` or a click picks one.
  - **Terminal:** the window in front, with its agent and run.
  - **AI Usage:** each agent's limits.
  - **CI:** the latest run of each workflow for the pane in front.
  - **Git:** stui has no git view yet, and the tab says so.
  - **Agents**, **History** and **Docs** are as before.

  The icons are drawn as pictures in Ghostty, kitty and WezTerm, and are one ASCII character elsewhere.
