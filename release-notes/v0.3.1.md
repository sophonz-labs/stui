**Typing reaches the session again after you click the panel.** In `stui --workspace`, a click on the right panel, or `ctrl-] 1`/`2`/`3`, gives the panel the keys. A click back on the session went to the program but left the keys with the panel, so what you typed went nowhere. A click on the session now gives it the keys back. `esc` and `ctrl-] tab` still do too.

**stui no longer spends your GitHub quota.** The CI marks in the lists read every pane's runs about every 10 seconds, with a fresh `gh run list` per repository each time. With a dozen repositories open, that is more than GitHub's 5,000 requests an hour, and every other tool using `gh` on the account then got rate-limited. Now:
- each repository is read at most once per 30 seconds, however many panes are in it;
- the read is a conditional request, and an unchanged list costs nothing from the quota;
- the lists ask for CI once a minute, and every 10 seconds only while the CI view is in front;
- when GitHub says the limit is spent, stui waits (1 to 15 minutes, or until the time GitHub gives) and shows the last runs it read.

Not checked in this release: the click fix in Ghostty by hand. A test covers it: it feeds a click, kitty-keyboard keys, focus reports and a bracketed paste, and checks they reach the pane.
